Compliance Statement

Evidence without overclaiming.

Avaran Circular Solutions Pvt. Ltd. supports organisations with waste traceability, material recovery, managed decommissioning, documentation and compliance-readiness support.

Our role is to improve visibility and evidence across the waste value chain. This may include material records, vendor documentation, movement tracking, evidence packs, closure reports and diversion summaries.

IMPORTANT BOUNDARY

Avaran is not a statutory authority, pollution control board, municipal body, legal advisor, auditor or ESG assurance provider.

Any report, certificate, summary or closure document issued by Avaran is based on the agreed scope, information available, client inputs, vendor records and project evidence. Such documents should not be treated as statutory certification, regulatory approval, legal compliance certification, environmental clearance, audit opinion, ESG assurance or a substitute for government filings.

Clients, vendors, recyclers, processors and transporters remain responsible for their own legal, regulatory and contractual obligations. Avaran does not support false recycling claims, forged documentation, unauthorised disposal or unsupported sustainability statements.
