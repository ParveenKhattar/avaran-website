WORKING WITH EXISTING VENDORS

Governance does not require replacing the ecosystem.

Avaran can work with the client's registered or existing vendors, subject to agreed governance, credentials, scope and evidence requirements. Where execution is required, Avaran can provide or coordinate the necessary service through approved partners.

This lets Avaran sit above the physical chain as the evidence, reconciliation and accountability layer while preserving practical delivery relationships.
