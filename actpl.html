Avaran Circular Traceability Protocol Layer

One protocol.
Multiple material streams.
Clearer outcomes.

ACTPL is Avaran's common technology and evidence backbone for capturing, linking and evaluating material events across all three businesses.

EVENT→EVIDENCE→OUTCOME

ACTPL maps material events, custody events, evidence, destinations, outcomes and exceptions.

THE CORE IDEA

From origin to closure, the chain remains connected.

ACTPL improves visibility across materials generated through demolition, decommissioning, fit-outs, IT refresh cycles and electronic asset disposal.

It records where material originates, how it is segregated, who handles it, where it moves and what final outcome is achieved.

ACTPL LIFECYCLE

Five connected stages.

01

Origin

Project location, category, quantity and date captured.

02

Registration & Segregation

Material batches are registered, classified and assigned a Batch ID with supporting evidence.

03

Movement & Collection

Transporter, vehicle, timestamps and destination are recorded.

04

Processing & Transformation

Authorised handlers, recyclers, refurbishers or processors and processing category are confirmed.

05

Outcome & Reporting

Final outcome is documented and closed through traceability reports, movement summaries and diversion insights.

+

Exceptions

Missing, late, inconsistent, unauthorised or implausible events remain visible for review.

+

Reconciliation

Dispatch-versus-receipt checks, variance and unresolved quantities are retained.

+

Closure

A concise closure statement and evidence pack appropriate to the contracted service level.

COMMON BACKBONE

ACTPL serves all three verticals.

DAAS

Execution traceability

Track material from site identification through dismantling, dispatch, receipt, recovery and project closure.

ENTERPRISE

Governance evidence

Create traceable evidence across client sites, vendors, transporters, recyclers and reporting periods.

PUBLIC SECTOR

System reconciliation

Reconcile events across contractors, facilities, ULBs, departments and downstream destinations; flag exceptions.

BUILD RULE

Technology follows a defined decision.

Every ACTPL feature is designed around a defined user, decision, evidence gap and practical use case within Avaran's three businesses. ACTPL is not a fourth business vertical.
