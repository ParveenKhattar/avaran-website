What We Do

Three businesses.
One evidence architecture.

Avaran's core proposition is governance, traceability and evidence. Execution services are available where required, either directly or through Avaran-approved or client-registered vendors.

GOVERN+EXECUTE+PROVE

ACTPL is the common technology and evidence backbone — not a fourth vertical.

01 / MANAGED MATERIAL SERVICES

Decommissioning & Managed Material Services

Avaran manages the physical end-of-life transition of assets, interiors, buildings and materials. The proposition is not demolition alone; it is decommissioning, value recovery, responsible routing and documented closure.

  • Site and asset inventory
  • Dismantling, demolition, segregation and site-clearance coordination
  • Reuse planning, salvage and scrap monetisation
  • E-waste, C&D and other stream routing
  • Transporter, recycler, processor and subcontractor coordination
  • Dispatch, receipt, quantity and destination evidence
  • Exception management and project closure reporting
  • ACTPL-based verified traceability and ACAR where contracted

DAAS Standard provides a defensible basic record. DAAS Verified adds digital custody events, reconciliation, exceptions, outcome data and ACAR.

CORPORATE EBID / TENDER / RFQ

Execution can open the door to governance.

Avaran may participate in corporate eBids, tenders and RFQs for demolition, decommissioning, shifting, dismantling, site clearance and related material services. Such opportunities may also provide an entry point for deployment of Avaran's material governance, traceability and evidence capabilities.

02 / ENTERPRISE GOVERNANCE

Enterprise Material Governance & ESG Evidence

Help enterprises govern and prove what happens to assets, materials and waste after they leave the organisation's immediate control. This can operate with or without Avaran managing the physical movement.

MGTA

Assessment

Identify gaps in evidence, vendors, custody and closure.

ACAR

Closure

VENDOR

Governance

Credential validation, scope checks, expiry monitoring and performance review.

Enterprise services also include ESG evidence support, EPR evidence integrity and ongoing programme oversight.

03 / PUBLIC SECTOR GOVERNANCE

Public Sector Material & Waste Governance

Enable authorities to see whether the material and waste systems they govern are functioning as reported — across contractors, facilities, jurisdictions and downstream destinations.

Customer groups

Environmental regulators · CPCB, SPCBs and PCCs

ULBs · Municipal corporations, councils and Nagar Panchayats

State missions / nodal agencies

PWD / CPWD / MES / development authorities

Smart City SPVs / ICCCs / state command centres

Public Asset Governance

Project-wise C&D accounting, contractor and transporter evidence, scrap inventory and custody, reusable-material records, dispatch-versus-receipt reconciliation and project closure statements — without necessarily becoming the works contractor.

Integration posture

Avaran sits above or alongside existing operational systems as an evidence, reconciliation and exception layer. API-first where possible; structured import/export where necessary; no generic replacement dashboard.

MINIMUM EVIDENCE STANDARD

Every engagement carrying the Avaran name should preserve a basic defensible chain: identity, origin, quantity, custody, destination, outcome, reconciliation, exceptions and closure.
