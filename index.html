Operating System for Circular Governance

From material movement to defensible evidence.

Undocumented material movement creates compliance, ESG and reputational risk. Avaran combines governance, evidence, technology and execution where required — from decommissioning and recovery through reconciliation, reporting and closure.

We are not simply a demolition contractor, recycler, dashboard provider or generic ESG consultant. ACTPL is the common technology and evidence backbone across Avaran's three businesses.
